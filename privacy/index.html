Privacy Policy

1. Your data lives in your browser

VaultFlow stores your subscriptions, prices, renewal dates, notes, and settings locally in your browser’s localStorage. We do not require an account and we do not maintain a cloud database of your subscriptions.

2. No bank connections & no selling data

VaultFlow never asks for your online banking credentials, card numbers, or read-only bank aggregator tokens. Bank statement CSV files and pasted SMS alerts are parsed entirely inside your browser tab and are never uploaded to our servers. We never sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. The only network requests VaultFlow makes

  • Daily exchange rates: Fetches public USD reference exchange rates from open.er-api.com at most once every 24 hours so multi-currency totals stay current.
  • Pro license verification: If you enter a Pro license key, VaultFlow verifies the key with [MoR NAME] (our merchant of record) to confirm your Pro status. Payment card details are handled exclusively by [MoR NAME] and never touch VaultFlow.
  • Cookie-less analytics (when enabled): Aggregate page counts and anonymous product milestones (such as completing setup or exporting a calendar). No cookies, no cross-site tracking, and never any subscription names, amounts, currencies, or notes.

4. Exporting and deleting your data

Because your data stays on your device, you have full control at any time. Open Settings inside the app to download a JSON backup, export CSV (Pro), or permanently erase all stored data with Clear all data.
